## Files
- tileset.png  — 16x16 worldmap terrain: grass / sand / snow coast transitions,
  forest, roads, bridges, town + castle + tower icons.
- mountains.png — grey and snow-capped mountain ranges (3x3 autotile layout;
  use the centre tile of each 3x3 block as the repeating mountain cell).
- water.png    — 480x48 animated water / coastline strip.
