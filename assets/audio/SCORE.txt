Adaptation and instruments
--------------------------
The game assigns the compositions to scenes and gives them display names.
tools/render_score.py renders their MIDI notes, tempo changes, program and
controller events offline with TinySoundFont, adds a short stereo echo, wraps
release tails for looping cues, balances levels, and encodes 32 kHz stereo
Ogg Vorbis. Victory and Elegy keep their release tails instead of looping.

Instruments: GeneralUser GS v1.471, by S. Christian Collins.
https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php
The GeneralUser GS SoundFont License v2.0 permits making music recordings;
it is separate from the compositions' licenses. Consult the bank's license
and sample-provenance note if substituting or redistributing a bank. The
SoundFont itself is not bundled or needed to play the game.
Bank used: GeneralUser.sf2, generaluser package 1.47.1,
SHA-256 f45b6b4a68b6bf3d792fcbb6d7de24dc701a0f89c5900a21ef3aaece993b839a

From the repository root, with mido, numpy, soundfile and tinysoundfont:
  python3 tools/render_score.py /path/to/GeneralUser.sf2
  python3 tools/render_score.py /path/to/GeneralUser.sf2 fen boss
The second form renders only the named cues. Normal builds and the art/SFX
generators use the checked-in score and do not re-render or replace it.
