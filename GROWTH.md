# Growth chronicle

## 2026-11-28 — the world, actually

The overworld was an infinite noise field with a lattice of guaranteed places
bolted on. It is now a **procedural-geography generator** in
`src/overworld_gen.cpp`: 320×320-tile continents built from seeded land lobes
with bays and capes, tapered wavy mountain ranges, stylised river splines,
lakes, biome fields and 2×2 forest copses. The homeland is generated by the
same rules as every other continent. Elmswick is the one fixed origin (the
runtime starts there); the other story places are rules, not coordinates — the
Castle of Elm seeks the northern crags, the Watchtower the north-west
shoulder, the Standing Stones the west, Hollow Hill the home woods — and the
seed picks the site. The named hamlets, the shrine, the gull cave, the east
spire and the boat-only Tidewell isles are placed the same way. Two seeds with
the same story produce visibly different continents (see
`docs/screenshots/world_seeds.png`).

Roads are A*-routed between sites with a turn penalty, so they run in country
straights instead of one-tile staircases; water becomes a bridge and a range
becomes a pass. Site pads are round clearings and **preserve the road**, so a
road enters a town gate instead of stopping two tiles short. Rivers are
stroked segment-by-segment (point-sampled splines left dry gaps and produced
zero bridges); river banks are grass, not beach; beaches only face the sea.
Mountain ranges are 2.15 half-width, so they read as ridges rather than a
wall of bricks. The one authored gesture is a northern range so the homeland
keeps the Lufia silhouette; its latitude, tilt, length and wave still come from
the seed.

The atlas is a grid of 320-tile cells, and the generator is pure per cell, so
the world has no outer edge: sailing into the next cell builds the next
continent, its roads and its sites. The preview now renders a 5×5 block of
cells (1600×1600 tiles) to show that (`docs/screenshots/world_atlas.png`), and
coast warp, forest fields and copse placement use **world** coordinates so two
cells never clone the same coastline or woods. The sailing mode also has the
[M] sea chart now, centred on the hull, because on open water the chart is the
only way to see where the next country is.

The runtime field renderer, collision, encounters, music mapping, the [M]
travelling map and the Journal's **Survey** page all read `owSampleTile` /
`owSitesInRect` from this one generator. The Survey page was rewritten: it
used to draw the retired noise field and disagreed with the ground under the
party's feet. Saves are v7; v4–v6 saves migrate and wake at Elmswick because
their world coordinates no longer describe the new atlas.

Verified headlessly (no GPU): `tools/verify_lufia_world.cpp` (homeland,
54 continents across 6 seeds, all sites walkable and reachable on foot,
Tidewell isles boat-only, roads/bridges/passes, outward continents, 106 ms
cold continent), `tools/verify_world.py`, `tools/link_check.py` and
`tools/validate_assets.py`. Previews in `docs/screenshots/` are rendered by
`tools/preview_world.py` from the real tile atlas and the real dump, not a
mock-up.
